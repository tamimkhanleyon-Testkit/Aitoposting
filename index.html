HeyMariner is an AI-powered maritime knowledge platform designed to become the daily companion of seafarers worldwide — bringing industry knowledge, regulations, learning resources, and maritime intelligence together into one platform.

Whether you are navigating international waters, studying at a maritime academy, managing a global fleet, or advising on compliance — HeyMariner is engineered for you.

The maritime industry has long relied on fragmented sources: flag state websites, IMO circulars, printed manuals, scattered forums. HeyMariner consolidates everything into a single, intelligent, always-updated ecosystem built for the realities of life at sea.

Mobile-first. Verified information. AI-enhanced. Available globally.
